# Infinity Outreach

Daily outreach checklists, pipeline tracking and gamified goals for **OptimAI** and **Nudge Digital**, in one app. Switch playbooks with the logo buttons in the sidebar; the pipeline, partners, XP, streaks and badges are shared.

## What it does

- **Dashboard**: greeting, an "Ask" box (try "What do I need to do today?"), weekly tiles, today's plan, targets, 14-day outreach chart, pipeline funnel, this week's content and upcoming follow-ups.
- **Search everything**: press `/` or Ctrl/Cmd+K to search both full playbooks, scripts, content ideas, prospects, partners and tasks. Questions are answered from your own data (no AI model is called).
- **Playbooks**: the complete Nudge Digital 90-day plan (22 sections) and OptimAI playbook (21 sections), searchable, with tables, [Verified]/[Assumption]/[Hypothesis] tags and one-click copy or "Save as my script" on every message template.
- **Scripts**: all playbook scripts, editable, with Personalise (fill the [placeholders]) and restore-original. Add your own.
- **Content**: 35 OptimAI and 29 Nudge posts, articles and newsletter ideas with drafts where the playbooks include them, status tracking (Idea, Drafting, Scheduled, Published), your own ideas, the content calendar, pillars and templates.

- **Today**: the 60-minute core and 120-minute extras for the active playbook, today's focus from the weekly rhythm, follow-ups due, scorecard counters, and "next working day's three actions".
- **Carry-over**: unfinished tasks roll into the next day automatically (shown as "Carried over from ..."). Tomorrow's three actions become tasks on the day. Missed core items from the last working day can be carried over with one click. Tasks can be snoozed to the next working day.
- **Pipeline**: prospects with source, signal, score (75+ approach, 55 to 74 nurture, under 55 drop), channel, Spam Act basis, stage history, notes and automatic follow-up dates (OptimAI day 4/10/21, max 4 touches; Nudge day 4/10, max 3).
- **Partners**: referrals sent and received, last contact, next action.
- **Progress**: levels, streak, 4-week heat map, weekly targets, custom goals, 26 badges, activity table, funnel.
- **Light, dark or auto theme**: switch at the bottom of the sidebar (remembered per device).
- **Review**: Friday review per playbook with a week picker, plus backup and restore.
- **Playbooks > Operating system**: weekly rhythm, rules, recurring tasks (weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly) and the launch checklist.

- **Other days**: on Today, use ← Yesterday / Tomorrow → or the date picker to look back (and tick things you did) or plan ahead. The dashboard's "This week at a glance" lists every day's focus and opens that day.
- **Weekly goals on the dashboard**: weekly targets plus your custom goals, with progress.
- **More detail dropdowns**: every core item, extra item and task has a "More detail" dropdown (what it means, steps, done-when, tips, links to the playbook section). The original text is unchanged.
- **Glossary**: underlined words (tracker, partner, qualified and so on) open a definition with links to the full playbook sections. Also under Playbooks > Glossary and in search.
- **Roadmap**: set Day 1 of each plan. Shows prep week, the current day/week, phases, weekly targets, what to do after day 90 and a one-click "start the next cycle".
- **Prospect contact details**: email, phone and LinkedIn (tap to email or call), searchable.
- **Goal measures**: each measure has a plain explanation. "Researched" counts automatically each time you add a prospect.
- **Mobile**: bottom navigation, 16px inputs, large tap targets, installable to the home screen.

"Today" is the date on your device and changes at midnight. Day 1 of the 90-day plan is set on the Roadmap page (default: the next Monday). After day 90 the Roadmap shows a review checklist and lets you start a new cycle on a date you choose.

- **Both playbooks on the dashboard**: "Today across both playbooks" shows OptimAI and Nudge side by side (focus, core 60 with live tick boxes, carried-over tasks, progress), plus any general tasks.
- **Start fresh**: Review > Start fresh clears prospects, partners, tasks, ticks, XP and badges (type RESET to confirm), lets you choose a new Day 1, and optionally keeps your edited scripts, content progress, own ideas and goals. Download a backup first.
- **Content library**: OptimAI (AI and automation for SMEs, making AI accessible) and Nudge (digital marketing for founders and startups) each have posts, founder posts, LinkedIn articles, Substack articles, email newsletters and website articles. Filter by type. All are drafts to edit with your own facts.
- **Ready to post**: 38 fully written drafts (filter "Ready to post"): per brand, 10 "me" posts for your founder profile, 6 company page posts and 3 complete articles, all edit-and-publish. In Lite mode the daily "Content out" card shows one in full (your post Mon/Wed/weekend, page post Tue/Thu, article Fri) with Copy and Mark published.
- **AI prompts**: Content > AI prompts has copy-ready prompts for Claude or any assistant (idea generation, post drafts, outlines, repurposing, voice, editing, calendars). Fill the [BRACKETS]; save your own prompts.
- **Consistent layout**: form fields, scorecards and tiles share one size and alignment.

- **Lite (travel) mode**: switch Full / ✈ Lite at the bottom of the sidebar (More on mobile). Lite strips the app to one page: a 30-minute "Lite 30" (connect with 5 people, message 5 people, after-connection follow-ups, replies, one discovery call) plus 30 optional minutes (research 5, content out, partner touch, tidy tracker), for whichever playbook you pick. It also shows today's numbers, follow-ups due, a content piece to post, tasks and quick add prospect. Lite ticks earn their own XP, count toward your streak and badges, and add to your real outreach counters, but never touch your 90-day plan days. While Lite is on the plan is paused; switching back shifts Day 1 forward by the days you were away, so the plan resumes exactly where you left it, with no missed-day carry-over.

- **Low-pressure scripts**: all 36 playbook scripts were rewritten to be shorter, specific, with one easy question and a graceful way out (no prices in cold messages), plus 7 new ones. Each has a "Why it works" note, and the Scripts page has a short guide to keeping messages low-pressure. Your own edits are kept; "Restore playbook original" now restores the new wording.

- **Dashboard by playbook**: every number is split OptimAI vs Nudge (tiles, weekly goals, daily focus, outreach chart, pipeline stages, follow-ups, content), with consistent rounded logos and playbook tags on tasks.
- **Lite dashboard and planning**: Lite has its own stripped dashboard for both playbooks, and you can step forward to tomorrow (or further) to see what is due and add tasks.
- **Find prospects**: copy-ready searches for LinkedIn (people, posts, jobs), Google, Google Maps, Seek and news, filled with your city and industry, each with where to type it and an Open link.
- **Prospect CRM**: the old "tracker" is now called the Prospect CRM everywhere, including the playbooks and glossary.
- **Network**: Partners became Network (stakeholders, clients, referral and agency partners, introducers) with type, which playbook it is for, and contact details.
- **Aligned daily tasks**: the two playbooks now share task names where they mean the same thing (Research and qualify, Outreach, Replies and follow-ups, Stakeholders and referrals, Prospect CRM).

It tracks and prompts only. It never sends messages.

## XP, in brief

Core task +10, extra task +15, full core +25 bonus, full 120-minute day +25 more, each counted action (request, email, reply, call and so on) a few XP, calls booked +20, stage progress up to +225 for a win, tasks +5, recurring and launch items +10, reviews +20. XP is calculated from your data, so un-ticking something removes its XP.

Streak = consecutive weekdays with a completed core 60 in either playbook. Weekends never break it.

## Run locally

```bash
npm install
npm start          # http://localhost:3000
```

Data is stored in `data/state.json` (and mirrored in the browser's localStorage, so it keeps working offline and syncs when back online).

## Deploy on Railway

1. Push this folder to a GitHub repo.
2. Railway: **New Project > Deploy from GitHub repo**. `railway.json` sets the start command and health check (`/healthz`).
3. **Add a Volume** to the service, mount path `/data`.
4. Service **Variables**:
   - `DATA_DIR=/data` (so your data survives redeploys)
   - `APP_PASSWORD=<choose a password>` (strongly recommended; the browser will prompt for it, any username works)
5. Settings > Networking > **Generate Domain**.

Without the volume, data resets on every deploy. Without `APP_PASSWORD`, anyone with the URL can read and edit your pipeline.

## Notes

- Dates use the device's local timezone (Melbourne for you).
- Single-user app. If two devices edit at the same time, the most recent save wins.
- `Review > Download backup` exports everything as JSON.
